By installing and using Emo Drink, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not install the app, or uninstall it and cease use.

1. What personal data we collect

Emo Drink collects the following categories of data:

1.1 Physiological and wellness data

Data read from your paired Garmin wearable via the Garmin Health SDK, including but not limited to:

  • Heart rate (resting, minimum, maximum, average, time-series, and beat-to-beat interval)
  • Respiration rate
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Skin temperature
  • Body battery / energy level
  • Step count, distance, floors climbed, and active minutes
  • Motion intensity and detected activity types
  • Stress level
  • VO2 Max and fitness age
  • Sleep data (total, deep, light, REM, awake; sleep score; naps)
  • Calories burned
  • Connected device status (battery level, firmware version, model)

1.2 Identifiers

  • A pseudonymous device identifier (Android ID) generated by the operating system. It is used to associate your physiological data with your app instance on our backend.
  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token, used solely to deliver push notifications to your device.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any account login credentials.

1.3 App interaction data

  • Your rating of drink recommendations (liked / disliked).
  • Your rating of emotion predictions (agree / disagree).
  • User preferences you choose in settings (e.g., age range, gender, sync duration, excluded drink categories).

1.4 Automatically collected diagnostic data

  • Basic app-usage events (app opens, session duration, screen views) and device characteristics (Android version, manufacturer, model, country, language) collected automatically by the Firebase SDK to help us understand service reliability and identify defects.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, photos, audio recordings, video, contacts, calendar, SMS messages, or call history.

2. How we collect it

  • Directly from you when you enter preferences or provide feedback in the app.
  • From your Garmin wearable, after you pair the device with Emo Drink. The app reads wellness data directly from your wearable over Bluetooth using the Garmin Health SDK. We do not access your Garmin account and do not receive your data through Garmin's cloud services.
  • Automatically from your device, for identifiers (Android ID, FCM token) and basic diagnostic telemetry.

3. Why we collect it

We collect and use your personal data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Emotion prediction. Your physiological data is processed by a machine-learning model to estimate your current emotional state (arousal and valence).
  • Drink recommendations. Based on the predicted emotional state and your preferences, the app suggests a beverage intended to complement your current physiology.
  • Model improvement. Your agree/disagree feedback on emotion predictions and drink ratings is used to improve the accuracy of the recommendation model.
  • Notification delivery. The FCM token is used exclusively to send you push notifications when a new recommendation is available.
  • Service quality. Diagnostic data helps us identify and fix defects.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing profiling, targeted content, or any purpose unrelated to the functioning of the app.

4. Who we share it with

We share your personal data only with the following parties, and only to the extent necessary to operate the app:

  • Our backend service, hosted on Google Cloud Run (Google LLC) in the United States. All physiological data and feedback is sent to this service for processing. The service is operated solely by Augmented Human Lab; no third party has access to it.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC) — used exclusively to deliver push notifications. Firebase does not receive your physiological data.
  • Firebase telemetry (Google LLC) — basic app usage events described in section 1.4. Firebase does not receive your physiological data or drink-recommendation feedback.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We do not share data with advertising networks, data brokers, or any third party outside the ones listed above.

5. International data transfer

Our backend is hosted in the United States (Google Cloud Run region us-central1). Firebase services are operated by Google LLC and may process data in data centres primarily located in the United States. In accordance with section 26 of the PDPA, before transferring your personal data outside Singapore we rely on Google Cloud Platform's standard contractual commitments and Google's independent certifications (including ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2), which we have assessed as providing a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

6. How long we keep it

Physiological data and interaction data are retained on our backend for seven (7) days from the time of collection, after which they are automatically deleted. Fully anonymised aggregates (from which no individual can be re-identified) may be retained beyond this period to support evaluation of the recommendation model.

Your Android ID, FCM token, and user preferences are retained on your device for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app removes these from your device. You may also request deletion of any backend records associated with your Android ID at any time (see section 8).

7. How we protect it

  • All data in transit between the app, our backend, and Google services is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Data at rest on your device is kept in Android's sandboxed application storage. Health data stored by the Garmin Health SDK is additionally encrypted on disk using SQLCipher.
  • Access to our backend is restricted to authorised members of the Augmented Human Lab team and protected by Google Cloud Identity and Access Management.
  • We will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore and affected individuals in accordance with PDPA sections 26B–26E if a notifiable data breach occurs.

8. Your rights under the PDPA

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore) you have the right to:

  • Withdraw consent. You may withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your personal data at any time by uninstalling the app or by contacting our Data Protection Officer. Withdrawal may affect the app's ability to provide recommendations.
  • Access. You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction. You may request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion. You may request that we delete backend records associated with your Android ID. We will action deletion within thirty (30) calendar days.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer using the details in section 13. We may ask you to provide your Android ID (available from the app's settings screen) so that we can locate your records.

9. Children

Emo Drink is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Android permissions we request

For transparency, here is why each Android permission is requested:

  • Bluetooth (scan, connect, advertise) — to discover and communicate with your Garmin wearable.
  • Activity recognition — required by the Garmin Health SDK to classify motion and activity types read from your wearable.
  • Post notifications — to deliver drink recommendations to your device.
  • Foreground service (connected device) — to keep the connection to your wearable active in the background for reliable periodic data sync.
  • Companion device — to use Android's native companion-device pairing with your watch.
  • Internet / network state / wake lock — to send data to our backend, receive recommendations, and complete data sync reliably.

We do not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, SMS, or call history. Legacy Bluetooth scanning on older Android versions is flagged with the neverForLocation attribute to indicate that we do not use Bluetooth scans to infer your location.

11. Health disclaimer

Emo Drink is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Emotion predictions and drink recommendations are intended for general wellness and exploratory use only. If you have any concerns about your health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional. Do not rely on this app to make decisions about your health or the health of others.
